# 5-Minute Demo Script - AI-Powered Study Planner

## Step-by-Step Demonstration

### 1. Goal Extraction & AI Parsing (1 Min)
- Navigate to **Goals & Subjects** page.
- Enter prompt: `"Physics exam on 20 Oct, weak in optics. Math exam on 25 Oct, difficult in calculus."`
- Click **Parse Goals with AI**.
- Review the extracted subjects, exam dates, topics, difficulty ratings, and estimated hours. Click **Save Subject to Database**.

### 2. Availability Setup (0.5 Min)
- Navigate to **Availability** page.
- Configure active study windows (e.g., Mon-Sun 18:00 - 21:00). Click **Save Weekly Availability**.

### 3. Plan Generation & Feasibility Warning (1.5 Min)
- Navigate to **Plan Calendar** page.
- Click **Generate Study Plan**.
- Highlight:
  - **Feasibility Banner**: Shows green checkmark for feasible plan or warning for overload with trade-off suggestions.
  - **AI Rationale**: Concise 4-line explanation of schedule ordering.
  - **Tailored Study Tips**: Active recall and spaced repetition advice.
  - **Day-Wise Timetable**: Clear view of Study vs Spaced Revision slots.
  - **Export .ics Calendar**: Download calendar file for Google/Apple Calendar.

### 4. Today View & Status Tick-Off (1 Min)
- Navigate to **Today** page.
- Select today's session, enter minutes spent (e.g. 60 mins), and click **Mark Completed** or **Mark Missed**.

### 5. Adaptive One-Click Re-Plan (1 Min)
- Navigate to **Progress** page.
- Review completion percentage chart.
- Click **Execute One-Click Adaptive Re-Plan**.
- Show how missed sessions are re-scheduled into upcoming days automatically with an AI explanation summary.
